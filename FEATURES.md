# Klinker: what the app offers

Klinker teaches 682 Dutch B1 words (62 places × 11 words) through an illustrated Amsterdam canal
city. Native SwiftUI, iPhone, iOS 17+, fully offline. All sounds and drawings are made in code.

Last updated: 2026-10-03.

## The learning engine
- [x] 682 words on 62 sheets, each with English, forms, partner words, an example sentence and its article (de/het)
- [x] FSRS-4.5 memory model per word: a quick repeat in the same session only nudges memory, a wrong answer brings the word back in 10 minutes
- [x] Five word levels: Nieuw, Gezien, Herkennen, Onthouden, Beheerst
- [x] Places open one at a time: the next opens once every word of the current one was answered right once
- [x] Place states: op slot, nu bezig, in aanbouw, gebouwd (all words remembered), verbleekt (3+ words slipping)
- [x] Rounds mix the current place's 11 words with up to 8 words due for review
- [x] Daily streak (days in a row with at least one answer)

## Games (rounds)
- [x] Je ronde: Match rush, then Ballonnen, then Knip & plak, then results (about 6 minutes)
- [x] Match rush: match 5 Dutch and 5 English cards against a 45-second clock, with combos
- [x] Ballonnen: hear a word, pop the balloon with its meaning before it floats away; faster with a combo
- [x] Knip & plak: build a Dutch sentence from cut-out strips (186 sentences, 3 per place), with a grammar tip
- [x] Every game can also be played on its own ("Losse spellen")
- [x] Results: each word's level dots grow or shrink, with a short note
- [x] Answers are saved straight away, so stopping halfway loses nothing

## Words
- [x] Calm word strips with article tape: blue = de, orange = het, grey = other
- [x] Word card: spoken word, forms, English, partner words, example sentence read aloud, level and "you remember this now for N%"
- [x] Fading words look sun-bleached and get a "bijna weg" tag

## De Stad: the home screen
- [x] The city map fills the screen: pan, three zoom levels (buttons or pinch), a button to jump to your current place
- [x] Map flies in from above when the app opens
- [x] Every place has its own building that shows what it is (bakery with bread, church with a spire, windmill, fire station with a fire engine…)
- [x] Places show their state: colour and a hanging shop sign (built), scaffolding and a crane (being built), grey with "!" (fading), closed shutters, a ribbon and a padlock (locked), "VEL n · VOLGENDE" on the next one
- [x] Tap a place to hear its name and open its card
- [x] Rows of canal houses, brick streets, arched bridges, houseboats, trees, bike racks, people and bikes
- [x] The countryside: tulip fields in spring, wheat in summer, cows, sheep on the dike, a turning windmill, beach parasols
- [x] Ink and watercolour style, paper grain, an old-map frame, district names along the ring, a "Klinkerstad" title panel and a wind rose
- [x] Light follows the real Amsterdam sunrise and sunset: pink dawn, day, golden hour, night with lit windows and street lamps
- [x] Shadows follow the sun
- [x] Seasons follow the calendar: blossom, autumn trees, snowy roofs, frozen canals with skaters
- [x] A Dutch drizzle now and then, with rings on the canals
- [x] Life on the map: cloud shadows, gulls, boats, a barge, sailboats, and Ria the postwoman cycling her round (tap her)
- [x] Red mailbox by the station with a count of new letters
- [x] Parties: a place opens (ribbon cut, padlock drops, applause), a place is built (scaffolding falls, its 11 words fly in, bells), a fading place is fresh again; with a banner, confetti and the place name spoken

## Little things to discover
- [x] 39 Amsterdam details at the closest zoom: black cats, the cat houseboat, ducks, geese, swans, a heron, a stork, a street organ, stroopwafel, fries, herring and cheese stands, a flower bike, a cargo bike, tour boats, pedal boats and more
- [x] Some only in one season (snowman, oliebollen, lambs, sun loungers…) or only by day
- [x] Tap one: it makes its own sound, says its Dutch word and shows it on a paper strip; finds are counted

## The Vandaag panel
- [x] Drag or tap the grabber: three heights
- [x] Small: today's new words and reviews, and the play button ("Gespeeld" once you've played)
- [x] Halfway: your current place, fading places, the letters and the house
- [x] Full: single games, your city in numbers, the postcards, your finds and your street

## A place's card
- [x] Gevelplaat: the building drawn large like an architectural plate, with numbered labels for its parts; tap to hear "de klokgevel", "het uithangbord", "de hijsbalk", "de wiek"…
- [x] Its 11 words, its state, progress and what to do next ("Speel je ronde", "Herstel", or what unlocks it)
- [x] "Ga naar binnen" (the memory palace room) and, for Jouw huis, "Richt je huis in"

## Memory palace rooms
- [x] All 62 places have a designed room where every word hangs on an object that means it
- [x] Verken: tap objects to find their words
- [x] Waar is…?: a spoken Dutch question, tap the right object (counts for memory)
- [x] Wat is weg?: one object disappears, pick the missing word (counts for memory)
- [x] The Gemeentehuis room is hand-made; Noor stands in the rooms

## De Anonieme Brieven (the letters story)
- [x] 62 anonymous letters, one per place, from a mystery writer "X"
- [x] Each letter arrives as an envelope with a wax seal and opens with an animation
- [x] Letters are ransom notes made of cut-out words; tap any word to hear it, "Lees voor" reads the whole letter
- [x] Ontcijfer: two questions per letter; solving it gives a clue
- [x] Dossier: corkboard with 4 suspects, clues pinned with red string, mark how much you suspect each one
- [x] The reveal of who X is after the last letter

## Jouw huis
- [x] Noor's canal house, outside and inside (four rooms)
- [x] 24 household objects (extra words) unlock as you finish places
- [x] Put them in their spot; each placement teaches staat / hangt / ligt / zit
- [x] Big furniture goes up on the hoisting beam and swings in through the window
- [x] Gezelligheidsmeter (cosiness meter) up to "Echt gezellig!"
- [x] Day and night; share a picture of your house

## Jouw straat
- [x] A canal street of the five real Amsterdam gable types, new every time ("Nieuwe straat")
- [x] Tap a house: its gable name, a bit of history, its gevelsteen word

## Ansichtkaarten (postcards)
- [x] The 62 places form 8 real Amsterdam neighbourhoods, grouped by where such places really are
- [x] When every place in one is open, Ria brings its postcard: the neighbourhood's buildings along a canal in Delft Blue, with one warm lit window
- [x] Turn it over for Ria's handwritten note; share it as a picture
- [x] The collection, with empty slots for neighbourhoods still to finish

## Sound and speech
- [x] Dutch words spoken with the on-device voice, offline, even on a silent phone
- [x] Sound effects made in code: a bike bell for a right answer, a soft bump for a wrong one, carillon bells for moments, applause, a music box for finds
- [x] Details have their own sounds: the street organ plays "In Holland staat een huis", boats toot, ducks splash, stands ring the till
- [x] A soft city soundscape: canal water, gulls, bike bells, the tram, birds in spring, crickets on summer nights, rain, winter wind, church bells on the hour
- [x] Respects the silent switch, keeps your music playing, separate switches for sounds and city sounds

## Accessibility
- [x] VoiceOver labels throughout (rooms describe objects without giving away the word)
- [x] Reduce Motion respected everywhere (no fly-in, no parties, still map)
- [x] 44-point tap targets; haptics for taps, right and wrong answers

## Settings (the ⋯ menu)
- [x] Dag en nacht: follows the clock, always day, or always night
- [x] Geluiden and Stadsgeluiden
- [x] Testmodus: every place and letter open, real progress untouched; adds "speel een feestje af" and "ansichtkaart"
- [x] Demo: jump to place 14
- [x] Begin opnieuw
